Toad Adventure — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Toad Adventure does not collect, store, or share any personal information. None. Not from anyone, and especially not from kids.

What the game collects

Nothing. Toad Adventure has no accounts, no sign-ins, no forms, no chat with real people, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We never see who plays the game or how they play it.

Game saves

Your toad's progress (XP, coins, pets, trophies, and friends) is saved only on your own device, using the device's local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes the save.

Internet use

The game is fully self-contained and plays offline. It does not talk to any servers, load ads, or send data anywhere.

Children's privacy

Toad Adventure was designed by an 8-year-old for players of every age. Because the game collects no information at all, there is no personal information from children (or anyone else) to collect, use, or share.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data exists to share. The game uses the open-source Three.js graphics library, which is bundled inside the app and makes no network connections.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's home page on GitHub: github.com/Magenta888cute/toad-adventure.
